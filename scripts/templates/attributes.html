Attributes

Every stylistic/structural attribute the engine tracks, from single fields up to media-wide categories. Each medium keeps its own native taxonomy for same-media comparison — click through below to see a book's own Style & Craft/Tone & Voice breakdown, or a short's own Title/Hook/Close/CTA mechanics. On top of that, both media are ALSO organized under one shared set of 14 macro categories — Diction, Rhetoric, Content Taxonomy, Delivery, Thesis & Purpose, Evidence & Authority, Tone & Voice, Structure & Organization, Target Audience, Bias & Assumptions, Argument & Reasoning, Context & Positioning, Style & Craft, and Readability — used only where a book and a short are being compared directly, never for same-media scoring.
